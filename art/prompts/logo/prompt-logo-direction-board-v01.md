## 完整提示词

Create a polished landscape concept direction board for the original Chinese casual game logo “暴躁打包员”. Use the three attached images only as style references: the worker sheet defines character comedy and line quality, the community warehouse scene defines warm industrial atmosphere, and the item board defines flat-cartoon rendering and palette. Do not copy any existing commercial logo or character.

Art direction: warm warehouse comedy, 2D exaggerated flat cartoon, simplified geometric shapes, thick dark outlines, bright warm colors, cute and irritable rather than threatening. Palette led by orange #F58A3A, deep orange #C9572C, yellow #FFD34E, cardboard brown #D6A257, cream #F6F1E0, industrial gray #697577, dark #2F383D, complaint red #E75353. No photorealism, no 3D render, no anime, no watercolor, no glossy mobile-game bevels.

Layout: one clean cream-background presentation board divided into three clearly separated logo directions, labelled A, B, C with tiny neutral labels outside the artwork. Each direction shows:
1) a main game title logo,
2) a small detachable graphic-symbol thumbnail derived from the logo,
3) one brief visual motif strip without extra words.
Keep ample whitespace and presentation-board clarity.

Direction A — “parcel burst badge”: compact stacked composition, rounded rough custom lettering squeezed inside a slightly crushed cardboard parcel/tape badge, small anger-vein and burst marks, playful and highly recognizable.

Direction B — “crooked cap tantrum”: horizontal composition, stronger mascot comedy; integrate a crooked warehouse-worker cap silhouette, angry eyebrows and a tiny steam puff around the lettering without turning it into a face-heavy mascot logo.

Direction C — “conveyor sprint”: dynamic forward-slanted horizontal composition, title riding over a conveyor-belt/tape stripe with motion marks, energetic arcade-game feeling, still warm and friendly.

Text requirement: the exact five Chinese characters “暴躁打包员” must appear once in each main logo, in that exact order. Do not omit, repeat, replace, or invent characters. Keep the characters large and legible; decoration must not cover the glyph structure. Do not add English, subtitle, watermark, mock phone frame, or unrelated text. This is a concept board, not a final runtime asset.
